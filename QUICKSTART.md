# Chess Vision

Close the old app and double-click Start Chess Vision.cmd.

Only Share screen, Stop, and the next move are shown.

Keep the full starting board visible on the primary screen, with your pieces at the bottom. Click Share screen before the first move. White/Black is detected automatically from the starting position. If you are White, the first suggestion appears. If you are Black, the app waits for White to move. Suggestions appear only on your turn.

Keep the app beside the board. Stop ends capture. After moving or resizing the game window, start a new share from the starting board. Joining halfway through a game is not supported by this automatic mode: the screen alone does not reliably reveal the turn or castling rights.

This version uses the supplied board theme and a basic local engine. It processes captures locally. Use for practice and permitted analysis.
